Explicit residual gates

The physical acceptance scope is unchanged:

  1. move the camera to the Pi and execute the prepared uncorrected preview and full-resolution V4L2 gates;
  2. acquire true blocked-light dark and translated/defocused blank-field sets, then repeat the physical Linux gate with the accepted calibration map;
  3. confirm Bayer phase, orientation, color response, and focus behavior with a known target; and
  4. complete the physical cable disconnect/reconnect case.

AVFoundation/system-wide macOS camera discovery remains a non-blocking deferral. Alpha 11 is a truthful provenance correction, not final acceptance.
